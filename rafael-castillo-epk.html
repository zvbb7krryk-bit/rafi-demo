The Story

Born to Tell Stories

Rafael A. Castillo is an Afro-Latino actor, writer, photographer, and storyteller from Miami, FL. His work is defined by one thing — authenticity. Whether embodying complex characters on screen or capturing raw human moments through his lens, Rafael brings a rare emotional honesty to everything he creates.

From the gritty streets of BMF on Starz to the heartfelt world of Mo on Netflix, Rafael has built a body of work that spans drama, comedy, and everything in between. His Dominican roots, his Miami upbringing, and his relentless self-belief shape every performance and every frame he shoots.

He doesn't just act. He observes. He documents. He feels. And then he brings it all to life on screen and through his photography — creating worlds that audiences don't just watch, but feel.
